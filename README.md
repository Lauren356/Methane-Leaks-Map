# Methane-Leaks-Map
This map provides a side-by-side comparison of methane leaks or plumes produced by oil & gas development and the amount of people exposed.

Data is taken from 2 sources:
Carbon Mapper data [2016-2025]. Retrieved from  https://data.carbonmapper.org  [Nov 8th, 2025]
U.S. Census Bureau. (2023). American Community Survey 5-Year Estimates, 2018–2023. Retrieved from https://data.census.gov

Click on the map for specific attribute pop-ups including bar charts of the number exposed by race/ethnicity or more info about the point source of CH4 (Methane). 

Of note is the classification of methane leaks by <100 kg/hr and >100kg/hr, representing which oil & gas sites are super emitters and which are not. The EPA defines a super emitter as producing > or = 100 kg/hr.
